**Research**

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Genetics at Rutgers University, supervised by [Dr. Jiekun (Jackie) Yang](https://www.yangcompbio.org). My research focuses on developing computational, statistical, and machine learning methods for single-cell, spatial, and multi-omics data, with the goal of uncovering disease mechanisms from high-dimensional biological datasets.

My current work centers on data-driven biological discovery from multi-omics data. I study complex diseases including melanoma and other cancers, and neurodegenerative disorders such as Down syndrome and Alzheimer's disease. I am particularly interested in using computational analyses to connect cellular states, tissue organization, and molecular programs to disease progression.

Methodologically, I work on emerging spatial transcriptomics and spatial multi-omics data, including cell annotation, cell-state inference, and cell-level reconstruction. These efforts aim to improve the resolution and interpretability of spatial omics analyses and enable more precise modeling of cellular organization in disease tissues.

**Previous Experience**

* Completed a Master's degree in Biomedicine at [Karolinska Institutet](https://ki.se/en), where I worked with [Prof. Jean Hausser](https://www.hausserlab.org/) on spatial modeling in triple-negative breast cancer and quantitative assessment of batch and biological effects in single-cell transcriptomics.

* Worked as a short-term visiting scholar in [Prof. Yi Zhang's lab](https://yi-zhang-compbio-lab.github.io/) at Duke University, where I co-developed STHD, a probabilistic machine learning method for cell typing in high-definition whole-transcriptome spatial data, now published in [Genome Biology](https://genomebiology.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s13059-025-03608-4).

* Developed single-cell and spatial transcriptomics analysis experience at the [Xuegong Zhang Lab](http://eca.xglab.tech/member/index.html) in the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University, studying cellular programs associated with atherosclerotic plaque instability and vascular remodeling diseases.

* Expanded from wet-lab research into computational biology through work at [AirTsinghua](https://air.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/) on cross-drug response evaluation, multi-omics data integration, and drug response modeling.

* Earned a Bachelor's degree in Life Sciences from [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), where I worked with [Prof. Yi Lin](http://linyi-lab.life.tsinghua.edu.cn/home/) on ATXN2/2L biology and contributed to a [Cell](https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674(23)00599-8) publication on circadian translation.
